# GATE CS question-bank audit

The ten supplied PDFs contain seven complete CS question papers and three answer-key documents. The bank has **455 distinct questions**: 2023 CS and 2024, 2025, 2026 CS1/CS2. Each paper retains 65 questions, 100 marks and a 180-minute practice duration.

The 2024/2025 supplied papers are scanned. Their official digital counterparts and final keys were obtained from the organizing institutes for reliable source images and scoring. The local files, download URLs and SHA-256 values are recorded in [official/sources.json](official/sources.json). The supplied 2023 and 2026 papers/keys are used directly. The extracted catalog preserves question number, type, marks, final-key text and source-page references.

## Authored solutions and topic coverage

All 455 worked explanations are authored in `scripts/author-*.mjs`. Answers are checked against the final keys; an independent arithmetic or diagram check is used where applicable. Final-key agreement is a validation condition, not proof that every explanation is error-free. Explanations are study material for this app, not official solutions from an institute.

`public/pyq/` preserves original question images, including diagrams and code layout. The interface presents them as the reference and also offers text transcription. Transcription can lose mathematical layout, so the original image remains authoritative. The extraction corrects labels vertically centred beside multi-line openings; the earlier crop omitted givens such as 4 MHz in 2024 CS2 Q11. Blank page space below the actual question is trimmed.

Each question has one primary lesson and up to two secondary mappings. The full bank reaches **131 of 150 concepts** (146 syllabus lessons plus 4 General Aptitude supplements). A topic with no direct PYQ in these supplied years is labelled as such; no past question is invented. [coverage.json](coverage.json) records exact primary and total counts per topic.

Every lesson has an expanded exam method, a worked problem, conditions/edge cases and an additional original numerical exercise. Additional depth now adds three varied MCQ/MSQ/NAT questions per topic and 44 transfer problems, for **794 original questions plus 455 PYQs (1,249 total)**. See [the full 455-question analysis](course-analysis.md) and [course review](../GATE_2027_REVIEW.md). The notes and additional questions are authored in `scripts/notes/` and `scripts/depth/`; no Gemini request is made while compiling, reading or practising this content.

## Scoring and mocks

- MCQ: full marks for an accepted choice, negative one-third of its marks for an incorrect attempted answer.
- MSQ: exact accepted sets only, no partial credit and no negative marks.
- NAT: inclusive final-key numerical intervals and alternatives, with no negative marks.
- Marks-to-all: full marks even when unanswered. Multiple officially accepted alternatives are preserved.

Seven full-paper mocks preserve original order. Three mixed mocks preserve 10 aptitude questions/15 marks and 55 CS/mathematics questions/85 marks, including 30 one-mark and 35 two-mark questions. The mixed mocks use disjoint question sets and draw across papers and primary topics. They are practice papers, not predictions of GATE rank or future topic weights.

Corrections include the 2023 key parser consuming a numerical 2 after the CS section name, negative signs separated from numbers in the 2026 CS2 key, NFA epsilon-edge direction in 2023 Q19, race-interleaving reasoning in Q38, and the regular language generated by Q39. Ambiguous final-key alternatives such as 2023 Q27/Q30 and 2025 CS1 Q60/Q65 are explicitly retained. Frequency-counting assumptions in 2025 CS2 Q47 are explained rather than hidden.

## Offline reproduction

```sh
npm run pyq:build
npm test
```

To rebuild source images from the local PDFs, run `npm run pyq:extract` first. The ordinary Vite/Vercel build consumes committed static files and never imports authoring tools. Gemini is reserved for explicitly submitted student doubts; notes and the entire question bank work without its key.
